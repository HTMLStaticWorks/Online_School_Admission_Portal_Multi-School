About the School

Oakridge International School is dedicated to providing a transformative educational experience. Our curriculum is designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and global awareness. With state-of-the-art facilities and a passionate faculty, we prepare students not just for college, but for life in a rapidly changing world.

Established in 1995, Oakridge has consistently ranked among the top 10 international schools in the region, boasting a 100% university acceptance rate.
