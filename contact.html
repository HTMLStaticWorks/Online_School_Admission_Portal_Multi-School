Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply to multiple schools?
Yes! EduPath is designed specifically for multi-school applications. You create one central profile, upload your documents to the Document Vault once, and you can apply to as many partner schools as you'd like without re-entering the same information.
How do I upload documents?
Once you log in to your Parent Dashboard, navigate to the "Document Vault". Here you can securely upload birth certificates, medical records, and past report cards. These documents will automatically be attached to any school application that requires them.
Are there any application fees?
EduPath is free for parents to use for browsing and tracking. However, individual schools may charge their own non-refundable application fees. These fees are displayed clearly on the school's profile page and during the final step of the application checkout.
How will I know if my child is shortlisted?
You will receive an email and SMS notification (if opted in) whenever there is a status update on your application. You can also view real-time status updates across all your applications in the "My Applications" tab of your dashboard.
