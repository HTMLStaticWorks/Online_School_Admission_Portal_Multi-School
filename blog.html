Frequently Asked Questions

The things parents ask us most about our guides and admission advice.

How often do you publish new articles?
We publish two to three guides a month, and we increase that during peak admission season (roughly September to January) when board deadlines and interview rounds come thick and fast. Every article carries its publication date so you can tell at a glance how current the advice is.
Is the admission advice specific to one board or city?
Most of our guides cover principles that hold across CBSE, ICSE, IB and State boards, and we label the ones that are board-specific in the article tag. Where fees, dates or document requirements vary by city, we say so in the piece rather than quoting a single figure.
Can I suggest a topic or ask a question of my own?
Yes — send it through the contact page and it goes to the same admissions team that writes these guides. Questions we hear repeatedly usually become a full article, and you will get a direct reply either way.
